# Super Scrubber - User Guide

## Welcome to Super Scrubber! 🧹

Super Scrubber is your intelligent real estate prospecting platform that automates business research, contact discovery, and MLS association lookup. Built specifically for dotloop/Zillow business consultants to streamline their prospecting workflow.

---

## Features Overview

### 🔍 **Multi-Source Data Aggregation**
- Searches across NAR directory, LinkedIn, company websites, and social media
- Cross-references data from multiple sources for accuracy
- Provides confidence scores based on source agreement

### 🤖 **AI-Powered Intelligence**
- LLM-based contact role categorization (broker, owner, office manager, admin, transaction coordinator)
- Intelligent deduplication of contact information
- Context-based inference for missing details

### 📊 **Comprehensive Business Profiles**
- Business verification with confidence scoring
- Key contact identification with role hierarchy
- State and local MLS association memberships
- Complete contact details (phone, email, website, address)

### 👥 **Team Collaboration**
- Shared access to search results
- Search history tracking
- Saved prospects library
- Automated notifications for high-value discoveries

### 🎨 **Elegant Design**
- Sophisticated navy and gold color scheme
- Intuitive dashboard layout
- Responsive design for all devices

## Getting Started

### 1. **New Search**

Navigate to the "New Search" page to begin prospecting:

**Search by any combination of:**
- Business Name
- Phone Number
- Email Address
- Website URL
- Street Address
- City, State, ZIP Code

**Tips:**
- You can fill in as few or as many fields as you have information
- The more details you provide, the more accurate the results
- Partial information works - the system will attempt to find matches

**What happens during a search:**
1. System searches NAR directory for official brokerage records
2. Scrapes company website for contact pages and team information
3. Checks LinkedIn for company profile and employee data
4. Aggregates social media presence
5. Uses AI to categorize contacts by role
6. Cross-references all sources for verification
7. Calculates confidence scores

### 2. **Understanding Results**

**Business Overview:**
- ✅ **Verified** - Multiple sources confirm the information
- ⚠️ **Unverified** - Limited source confirmation
- **Confidence Score** - Percentage based on source agreement (higher is better)

**Key Contacts:**
- **Primary Contact** - Usually the broker/owner
- **Role Badges** - AI-categorized roles:
  - Broker
  - Owner
  - Office Manager
  - Admin
  - Transaction Coordinator
  - Technology POC
- **Role Confidence** - How certain the AI is about the role assignment

**MLS Associations:**
- **State** - State-level association membership
- **Local** - Local MLS/board affiliations

**Data Sources:**
- Shows which sources contributed to the profile
- Common sources: NAR, LinkedIn, Company Website, Social Media

### 3. **Search History**

View all your past searches:
- Click any search to view full results
- See search status (completed, pending, failed)
- Check processing time
- Review result counts

**Useful for:**
- Revisiting previous prospects
- Tracking your prospecting activity
- Sharing results with team members

### 4. **Saved Prospects**

Your library of discovered businesses:
- All businesses from successful searches are automatically saved
- Quick access to complete profiles
- Filter and organize your pipeline
- Export contact information

**Best Practices:**
- Review saved prospects regularly
- Add notes about outreach attempts
- Share high-value prospects with your team

### 5. **Notifications**

Stay informed about important discoveries:

**Notification Types:**
- 🎯 **High Value Prospect** - Businesses matching your target criteria
- ✅ **Verification Complete** - Search finished with verified data
- 👥 **Team Share** - Colleague shared a prospect with you
- 🎲 **Criteria Match** - Automated monitoring found a match

**Managing Notifications:**
- **Mark as Read** - Individual notification management
- **Mark All Read** - Clear all unread notifications
- **Send Test** - Verify notification system is working

## Advanced Features

### Multi-Source Verification

Super Scrubber doesn't rely on a single data source. Instead, it:

1. **NAR Directory** - Official REALTOR® association records
2. **LinkedIn** - Professional profiles and company pages
3. **Company Websites** - Direct source for contact pages
4. **Social Media** - Facebook business pages, Twitter profiles
5. **Web Search** - Google search for additional mentions

**Confidence Scoring:**
- 90-100%: Excellent - Multiple sources agree
- 70-89%: Good - Most sources confirm
- 50-69%: Fair - Some sources confirm
- Below 50%: Low - Limited confirmation

### AI Contact Categorization

The LLM analyzes:
- Job titles and descriptions
- Context clues from website content
- LinkedIn profile information
- Common industry patterns

**Role Detection:**
- **Broker/Owner** - Decision maker for technology purchases
- **Office Manager** - Often handles administrative tech decisions
- **Transaction Coordinator** - End user of transaction platforms
- **Admin** - Support staff who may influence decisions
- **Technology POC** - Dedicated IT or tech contact

### Team Collaboration

**Sharing Results:**
- All searches are automatically available to your team
- Search history is shared across team members
- Saved prospects are accessible to everyone

**Best Practices:**
- Add notes to prospects for context
- Use notifications to alert team of high-value finds
- Coordinate outreach to avoid duplicate contact

## Tips for Success

### 🎯 **Effective Searching**

1. **Start Broad, Then Narrow**
   - Begin with business name and city
   - Add more details if results are too broad

2. **Use Partial Information**
   - Don't wait for complete data
   - The system can work with fragments

3. **Check Confidence Scores**
   - Focus on prospects with 70%+ confidence
   - Lower scores may need manual verification

### 📞 **Contact Strategy**

   - Start your outreach here

2. **Understand the Hierarchy**

   - LinkedIn profiles help personalize outreach

### 🔄 **Workflow Integration**

3. **Team Sync**
   - Share promising prospects

## Troubleshooting

### **No Results Found**

**Possible Reasons:**
- Business name spelling variation
- Business recently established (not in databases yet)
- Limited online presence

**Solutions:**
- Try alternate business name spellings
- Search by phone or email instead
- Use just city and state for broader results

### **Low Confidence Scores**

**Why This Happens:**
- Conflicting information across sources
- Limited online presence
- Common business name causing confusion

**What To Do:**
- Manually verify using the provided website link
- Check LinkedIn directly
- Use additional search parameters

### **Missing Contact Information**

**Common Causes:**
- Business doesn't publish contact details publicly
- Information behind login walls
- Privacy settings on social media

**Workarounds:**
- Use NAR directory link to find association contact
- Check state real estate board listings
- Call main business number to request POC
